books.

A sequence of books for professionals and leaders who sense that the gap between the life they are living and the life they intended is wider than it should be — and who are ready to close it.

The premise

Not aspirational self-help. A clear-eyed examination of how compounding works in a human life — and what to do about it today.

Two books.

The series
01

Book one

Lead With You

Begins at the root: purpose. It asks who you are leading from, not just who you are leading toward, and offers a framework for building the kind of self-knowledge that makes every subsequent decision cleaner.

What the series addresses

Understand your why

The author

Ravi Sibal

The Course Correct series is written for the person who is serious about their own growth and willing to be honest about where they are. The books examine how compounding works in a human life — in careers, decisions, and the slow accumulation of deferred choices — and what it takes to reverse course before the window closes.
