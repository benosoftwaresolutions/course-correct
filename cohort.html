The problem

Most people don't fail because they lack ambition. They fail because they never stop to ask: whose definition of success am I chasing? Course Correct is where that question gets answered — and turned into a plan.

10 weeks.

What's inside
Week 01–02

Lead with You

Define your personally held version of success — not your parents', not your industry's. The foundation everything else is built on.

Week 03–04

Audit the Decade

An honest, structured review of where you are today — in career, relationships, finances, health — and the gap you're closing.

Week 05–06

Focus on Why

Reconnect with your driving motivations before committing to any direction. Strategy without purpose collapses under pressure.

Week 07–08

Finish with How

Build your 10-year map — milestones, pivots, non-negotiables, and a 90-day action plan you can execute the week you finish.

Week 09–10

Course Correction Playbook

Tools, rituals, and accountability structures to stay on track when life inevitably pulls you sideways.

Ongoing

Cohort Community

A private group of peers navigating the same crossroads — the most underrated part of any serious programme.
